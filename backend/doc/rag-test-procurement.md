# RAG Test Procurement Knowledge

> This document is synthetic test data for validating the ERP RAG pipeline.

## Brake Pad Compatibility

Part number `BP-100` is compatible with the `MP-2024` motor platform. The
recommended minimum order quantity is 20 sets. A standard set contains four
pads and one wear sensor.

## Supplier Lead Time

Supplier `Northwind Components` normally ships `BP-100` within five business
days after an approved purchase order. Orders above 500 sets require manual
confirmation of the delivery schedule.

## Quality Inspection

Incoming brake pads must pass visual inspection, dimension sampling, and wear
sensor continuity testing. A failed batch must be placed on hold and cannot be
released to inventory until the quality team records a disposition.
